# Lumora Care: owner handbook

Lumora Developments is a fictional UAE property developer used for this demo. All policies, figures and contact details below are illustrative.

## Communities

- **Lumora Creek, Dubai.** Three residential towers (A, B and C) with 612 apartments, from studios to three-bedroom units. Tower A and Tower B are completing handovers in 2026; Tower C is under construction. Unit numbers look like CRK-A-1204 (Tower A, apartment 1204).
- **Lumora Oasis, Abu Dhabi.** 180 townhouses and 96 villas, handed over between 2024 and 2026. Unit numbers look like OAS-TH-031 (townhouse 31) or OAS-V-112 (villa 112).

## Contacting Lumora Care

- Care line (voice agent and care team): every day, 24 hours for emergencies; care team available 8 am to 8 pm, Monday to Saturday.
- Lumora app and owner portal: payments, documents, tickets and appointment changes at any time.
- Handover centres: Lumora Creek Handover Centre (ground floor, Tower A) and Lumora Oasis Community Centre. Open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, by appointment only.

## Handover

Handover is when the owner receives the keys and the unit becomes their responsibility. The steps are:

1. **Handover notice.** Lumora sends a notice when the building has its completion certificate. It lists anything the owner still has to complete.
2. **Readiness items.** Before an appointment can be booked, the owner must have: paid the final instalment in full; paid the first service-charge advance; registered the unit with the local water and electricity provider and shared the account number with Lumora; and signed the handover documents in the owner portal.
3. **Appointment.** Once every item is complete, the owner books a 90-minute appointment at the handover centre. Appointments can be moved up to 24 hours before without charge through the app or the care line.
4. **Inspection and keys.** The owner inspects the unit with a Lumora engineer, lists any snags on the inspection form, and receives keys and access cards.

**What to bring:** original Emirates ID or passport; a notarised power of attorney if someone attends on the owner's behalf; and the handover notice (printed or in the app).

**Snags found at handover** are fixed within 30 days. The owner does not need to call about snags already on the inspection form.

## Defects liability period (DLP)

- Every unit has a **12-month defects liability period** starting on the handover date.
- During the DLP, Lumora repairs defects in workmanship and materials at no cost: finishes, plumbing, electrical, air conditioning, doors and windows, and built-in appliances supplied with the unit.
- The DLP does not cover wear and tear, damage caused by occupants or their contractors, items the owner added or changed, or consumables such as light bulbs and filters.
- Structural elements are covered for ten years from completion under UAE law.
- After the DLP ends, repairs are done by Lumora Facility Services on a chargeable basis: a visit fee of AED 150 plus parts and labour, quoted before work starts.

## Maintenance response times

| Urgency | Examples | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | Active flooding or major leak, fire or smoke, gas smell, sparking or exposed live wiring, lift entrapment | Technician on the way within 2 hours, 24/7 |
| Urgent | No air conditioning in summer, no water supply, a contained leak, a front door that will not lock | Within 24 hours |
| Routine | Cracked tiles, a loose cabinet door, paint or sealant touch-ups, a dripping tap | Within 5 working days |

For fire or a gas smell, leave the unit and call Civil Defence on 997. For injuries, call an ambulance on 998. For police, call 999. For water leaks, turn off the main water valve (usually under the kitchen sink or in the utility cupboard) and keep clear of electrical sockets.

## Service charges

Service charges pay for running and maintaining the shared parts of the community. They cover: security and CCTV; cleaning of common areas; landscaping; maintenance of lifts, pools, gyms and play areas; common-area utilities; building insurance; community management; and a reserve fund for major replacements. They do not cover utilities inside the unit, district-cooling consumption charges, or repairs inside the unit after the DLP.

- Charges are calculated as an annual rate per square foot of the unit's area, set by the community's approved annual budget. Lumora Creek: AED 16.50 per square foot. Lumora Oasis: AED 5.20 per square foot.
- Invoices are issued annually in January, payable in full by 31 January or in four quarterly instalments (31 January, 30 April, 31 July, 31 October) through the app.
- Ways to pay: the Lumora app, the owner portal, or bank transfer quoting the unit number. Lumora never takes card payments over the phone.
- If a balance is overdue, the owner receives reminders at 7, 30 and 60 days. Under community rules, amenity access cards may be suspended when a balance is more than 90 days overdue.
- Owners who need a payment plan or want to dispute a charge can ask for a callback from the finance team, who reply within 2 working days.

## Buying, renting and reselling

Questions about buying a new unit, renting, or reselling go to the Lumora sales team through a callback. The care line does not quote prices or availability.
